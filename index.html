Panorama Education · AI

AI Survey Insights

One of Panorama's first user-facing AI features. It surfaces the most actionable findings in large survey datasets, weighs scores against national benchmarks and past results, and links every insight back to its source data. I tested output with Product, Data Science and Design for accuracy and neutral tone, because educators act on it.

Panorama Education · Reliability

Reporting stability

The flagship reporting product was hitting urgent issues about weekly from a third-party analytics engine. As incident commander, I used AI to learn the engine's internals quickly, then fixed the root cause by combining redundant requests and cutting unnecessary traffic.

No comparable incident in the months that followed; recognized by engineering leadership.

Epic Systems · Clinical

Vaccine inventory rebuild

A major clinic customer was considering uninstalling Epic's vaccine inventory tool. I made the case for rebuilding it on a separate inventory platform, split the work so a growing team could build in parallel, and wrote the data conversion and rollback utilities myself.

Shipped February 2022; the same customer praised the "incredible improvements."

Epic Systems · Clinical

Medication record, desktop to web

Key developer on moving the medication administration record to the web, one of Epic's largest projects. I redesigned data loading to one database call per screen, designed and ran the usability study; and planned a thorough QA process that caught 93 issues internally, then owned resolving them before customers ever saw them.

93 defects resolved in six months after release.
